Our Story

Connecting breeders in Butuan City through technology, trust, and a shared passion for animal welfare.

Why We Started

BreedLink was born from a simple observation: people in Butuan City struggled to find trusted breeding partners for their pets, relying on scattered social media posts — making it hard to know if an animal is healthy and safe.

As students, we created this platform to solve that problem. We envisioned a digital hub where pet owners and breeders can list their animals, upload vaccination and health documents, and easily find a suitable breeding partner.

We're proud to help pet owners and breeders in Butuan City connect with confidence and make informed decisions about their animals.
